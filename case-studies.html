Case study 01

Full-funnel performance campaign: from awareness to conversion

Client
Client or industry
Role
Paid media strategist & buyer
Channels
Meta, Google Search, Display
Timeline
e.g. 6 months

The challenge

The client was spending on paid media but couldn't tie it to revenue. Campaigns were split across platforms with inconsistent tracking, broad targeting and no clear path from first impression to conversion.

Tailor this to the real situation: what was the budget, the goal and the problem?

My approach

  • Measurement first: audited pixels, conversion events and UTMs so every channel reported against the same goals.
  • Funnel structure: rebuilt campaigns into awareness, consideration and conversion stages, each with its own audiences, creative and KPIs.
  • Audience strategy: layered lookalikes, interest and intent audiences with retargeting pools fed by upper-funnel engagement.
  • Test and reallocate: ran structured creative and landing-page tests, then moved budget weekly toward the best-performing ad sets.

Case study 02

Meta Ads 101: teaching paid social at Toronto Metropolitan University

Organization
TMU, The Creative School
Role
Workshop designer & facilitator
Format
Hands-on workshop
Audience
e.g. 30 graduate students

The challenge

Digital media students learn a lot of theory about platforms and algorithms, but rarely get to see how a real paid campaign is set up, funded and optimized. The goal was to turn more than a decade of media-buying experience into something students could use right away.

My approach

  • Built the session around a real campaign workflow: objective → audience → creative → budget → measurement.
  • Walked through Meta Ads Manager live, showing how campaign structure, targeting and bidding choices affect results.
  • Used real-world examples to show what separates campaigns that convert from campaigns that only spend.
  • Finished with a practical checklist students could apply to their own projects.

Outcome

The workshop led to a proposed three-part series for the Master of Digital Media program: (1) formulating a digital media campaign from awareness to conversion, (2) Meta Ads 101, and (3) digital media analytics.

Case study 03

Strategy meets code: building the pages the media points to

Context
Web development coursework
Role
Designer & developer
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, C#
Code
github.com/zabocaman

The challenge

Great targeting can't save a slow or confusing landing page. As a media buyer, I kept seeing that conversion problems were often really website problems, so I decided to learn to build the pages myself.

My approach

  • Completed web development workshops at Humber College, covering semantic HTML, responsive CSS and accessibility.
  • Built Brewed Coffee, a responsive brand site, and jspet, an interactive JavaScript project.
  • Built a server-side C# back-end project to understand how data, forms and tracking work behind the scenes.
  • Built this portfolio site by hand.

Outcome

I can now talk to developers in their own terms, prototype landing pages quickly and spot conversion blockers before ad budget gets spent on them. It also sets up my next step: combining media strategy with AI and interactive systems.
