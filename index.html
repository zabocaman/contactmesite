About me

Where strategy, creativity and technology meet.

I started out studying people, with a degree in Psychology and Communication Studies from the University of the West Indies in Trinidad and Tobago. That curiosity about how people decide still drives how I plan media.

Since then I've earned an MBA in Marketing from the University of Bedfordshire (UK), worked agency-side as a PPC specialist, and managed more than $10M USD in paid campaigns. Today I'm based in Toronto and completing a Master of Digital Media at Toronto Metropolitan University. My research looks at how AI, data-driven platforms and algorithms are reshaping communication, culture and decision-making.
